SUBJECT: Tarver, Josh J.
DATE OPENED: 04/12/1994
DOB: 3/9/1964
STATUS: ACTIVE INVESTIGATION
FIG 1. TARVER, J. (199█)

[EXTRACT FROM CASE RECORD]: Criminal proceedings were initiated against civilian Josh J. Tarver on charges of first-degree murder, attempted murder, aggravated battery, unlawful practice of medicine, illegal possession of a firearm, and operating an illegal gambling establishment. All criminal charges and civil lawsuits were subsequently dismissed in court.

DISTINGUISHING MARKS: Severe craniofacial deformity, complete absence of ocular globes despite retained visual perception, multiple fractured teeth.

An infamous figure within the criminal underworld who built his reputation on unsettling honesty and pathological gambling associated with a game of his own design—a modified version of Russian Roulette played with a 12-gauge Remington 870 pump-action shotgun. He primarily operated out of a backroom in the BLACKWATER nightclub (City of NEW HAVEN), yet consistently evaded prosecution due to notarized General Release of Liability waivers signed voluntarily by participants who were effectively walking to their deaths.

The exact nature of Tarver himself remains undetermined. Documented incidents confirm the subject has survived point-blank buckshot blasts to the head without utilizing the resuscitation equipment he personally installed for his players. Despite his grotesque appearance, the subject is noted to be remarkably polite, albeit largely taciturn.

Those who perish during his games do not vanish without a trace: Tarver harvests blood from the deceased, categorizes it by blood type, and uses it as donor stock to resuscitate subsequent participants.

CASE ADDENDUM — DATED 09/17/199█

Investigation revealed that prior to his criminal activities, Josh J. Tarver worked as a certified electrical engineer. He was legally married to ELENA TARVER, until a tragic incident on 11/29/1991 left him widowed and alone.

Falling into a severe clinical depression, Tarver attempted suicide using a Remington 870 shotgun, but the chambered shell turned out to be a blank. Following the misfire, all suicidal attempts ceased.

After purchasing a utility room on the upper level of the BLACKWATER nightclub, Tarver retrofitted it into an isolated gaming chamber of his own engineering. With each match played, his physical appearance deteriorated drastically: he lost his eyes and several teeth, and his skull became severely deformed. However, the subject showed no signs of physical pain and continued hosting games unimpeded.

FIELD REPORT — DATED ██/07/199█

Josh J. Tarver vanished without a trace in the middle of a match. According to the testimony of the sole surviving witness:

"That FUCKING son of a bitch just vanished into thin air right in front of my eyes, MAN! Just disappeared without a trace, like he melted into smoke!!!"

Toxicology screening of the witness's blood revealed no traces of narcotics, psychotropics, or alcohol. Follow-up interrogations are ongoing.

According to current intelligence, Tarver disappeared under unknown circumstances, leaving behind large sums of cash, personal identification, and his vehicle. The investigation remains active.
